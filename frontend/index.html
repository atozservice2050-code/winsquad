📜 Deposit & Withdrawal Policy

1. Deposits are credited after manual approval — always keep your transaction ID (TID).
2. Before your first withdrawal, your account must be verified (CNIC) — see Accounts above.
3. You must play (bet) at least your total deposited amount before you can withdraw.
4. Withdrawals are paid only to your own saved account — never to a third person's wallet.
5. Wallet withdrawals are processed within 24 hours. Limits: Rs 10,000 – 50,000, max 50% of your withdrawable balance, once per day.
6. Game rewards are credited as locked bonus. Bonus unlocks as you play — every rupee you bet unlocks one rupee of bonus. Once unlocked, it is fully withdrawable like your own money.
